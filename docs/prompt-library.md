# Prompt library

Copy, swap the brackets, attach the images the prompt refers to. Attribution is the person who first shared the prompt. Most of the viral ones are archived in [PicoTrex/Awesome-Nano-Banana-images](https://github.com/PicoTrex/Awesome-Nano-Banana-images) and [ZeroLu/awesome-nanobanana-pro](https://github.com/ZeroLu/awesome-nanobanana-pro) with result images.

## Photorealism

**Portrait with full photographic spec**

```
A close-up portrait of an elderly fisherman on his boat at golden hour, shot
on an 85mm lens at f/1.8, shallow depth of field with creamy background
bokeh. Loop lighting from the low sun creates a subtle nose shadow and a warm
rim light along his silver stubble. Kodak Portra 400 color: warm skin tones,
gentle highlight roll-off, fine visible grain. Rule-of-thirds composition
with his eyes on the upper intersection, negative space of open water to
his left. 4:5.
```

**Night exterior where the film stock does the work**

```
Wide establishing shot of a rain-soaked neon alley at night, 24mm lens with
deep depth of field, from the foreground puddle reflections to the far
signage all in focus. Cinestill 800T signature: cold blue-tungsten cast with
red halation blooming around the neon. Every light source is a practical in
frame, wet asphalt reflecting magenta and cyan. Slight Dutch angle, the
converging alley walls leading to a lone figure walking away. 21:9.
```

**Long exposure**

```
Long-exposure night photograph of a city intersection from a high angle,
quarter-second shutter drag: headlights and taillights smear into continuous
trails while a street vendor's cart stays frozen sharp from a flash pop.
Teal-and-orange grade, cool shadows in the wet asphalt, warm sodium
streetlight highlights. Bird's-eye framing, diagonal leading lines from the
crosswalk stripes, an out-of-focus traffic light occluding the bottom
corner. 16:9.
```

**Deliberately ordinary phone selfie** (attributed to @jiamimaodashu, one of the biggest trends of 2025)

```
An extremely ordinary, unremarkable iPhone selfie with no clear subject or
sense of composition, like a snapshot taken by accident. Slight motion blur,
uneven lighting from the sun or shop lights causing mild overexposure,
awkward angle, cluttered framing. The overall feel is deliberately mundane,
as if the phone went off while being pulled out of a pocket. The subject is
[person] at night next to [landmark].
```

**Reset the camera to a specific exposure** (@hckinz)

```
RAW-ISO 100, f/2.8, 1/200, 24mm settings.
```
