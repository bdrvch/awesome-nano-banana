# Vocabulary

What each term does to the image. Google's own guide confirms named cameras and stocks work as style shorthand rather than as technical settings: GoPro reads as "immersive, distorted action", Fujifilm as "authentic color science", a disposable camera as "raw nostalgic flash". Use the words for the effect you want, not for accuracy.

## Named artists: translate the name into attributes

It works better (the name overfits to one famous work), it survives a model switch, and it stays clear of a moving policy line. Google's use policy bars deceptive impersonation and IP violations, not style imitation as such, but Disney sent Google a cease-and-desist over Gemini output in 2025 and filters shift.

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| in the style of Moebius | clean ligne claire linework, flat desaturated ochre and dusty teal, vast alien landscapes dwarfing tiny figures, fine hatching on machinery and rock |
| in the style of Ghibli | soft cel-shaded characters, hand-painted watercolor backgrounds, rounded warm features, lush detailed foliage, gentle diffused daylight |
| in the style of Van Gogh | thick swirling impasto with visible directional ridges, cobalt blue against golden yellow, turbulent expressive sky |
| in the style of Giger | biomechanical fusion of organic and machine, dark chrome-and-bone palette, airbrushed metallic sheen |
| in the style of Rockwell | warm mid-century Americana staging, soft realistic oil texture, narrative genre scene, golden-hour light |
| in the style of Mucha | Art Nouveau flowing line, decorative floral halo, pastel jewel tones, elongated hair and drapery, ornamental border |
| in the style of Wes Anderson | symmetrical centered composition, pastel color-blocked palette, flat frontal framing, quirky vintage production design |
| in the style of Kusama | repetitive polka-dot fields, saturated primaries, infinite mirrored repetition, soft biomorphic forms |
